# Justin — Brief

2026-11-24 · draft for Justin's review

**Draft.** A preview site Justin can open beside v2. It shows him as someone who
does AI for construction: the skills that come from that work, the systems he
has built, writing on AI in construction, and a way to talk about consulting,
speaking or advising. Construction OS is one piece of the work, with a link
out, and the page is about him.

Justin, 2026-11-24: "the new branch should be like 100% focused on ai for
construction as something i do. we can figure out the domain later, i'm just
going to want to see the demo."

## 1. Customer

Someone who wants Justin in the context of construction, and Justin himself,
looking at this demo next to v2.

- **A construction owner or operations leader** who met him, or followed his
  link, and wants to know who he is, what he has built for construction, and
  how to start a conversation.
- **Justin**, comparing this preview with the v2 site and deciding what he
  wants to keep.

**Who to test a decision against:** the owner of a mid-size residential builder,
or an operations leader at one, who already has his link. The same reader the
[Construction OS brief](../construction-os/brief.md) calls the buyer, met here
as a person looking for Justin rather than as a buyer of software.

**Who it is for, stated the other way around so the scope stays put:**

- Someone hiring an enterprise AI product leader, a recruiter placing a C2C
  contract, or a reader of general AI writing. That is the v2 site's audience.
- Someone ready to buy Construction OS from this page. They get a link to the
  product site. The offers stay there.

## 2. The painful moment

A construction owner who has met Justin follows his link and lands on the
Construction OS site at justinyurasits.com: pain points, four modules, and
Audit, Build and Monthly. The short founder note is inside a product pitch.
The owner cannot see, in one place, the person who does AI for construction,
the work, and a way to talk that is not "Book a demo."

v2, designed in [justinyurasits-com/](../justinyurasits-com/), answers a wider
version of that moment. Its working headline is "AI Product Leader. Systems
Designer. Hands-On Builder." and it gives equal weight to enterprise AI and
construction. This pilot answers only the construction version, so Justin can
look at that answer on its own.

## 3. What it replaces

For the reader of the demo, it replaces "the only page about Justin is a
product pitch." It does not replace the live site, and it does not replace v2.
Both stay where they are. The domain is a later decision.

## 5. The wedge

The first slice is the preview itself. Home, story, three case studies
(Construction OS, the change order generator, Fieldscope), a writing library
with nothing published yet, work with me, and contact. Neutral styling, so the
comparison with v2 is the content and the structure.

It comes first because Justin said he wants to see the demo, and the domain
can wait. A foundation that does not render pages does not meet that.

## 6. Out of scope, on purpose

- **The domain, DNS, and any cutover.** He will decide that later. This pilot
  does not take justinyurasits.com, does not merge into `main` or `v2`, and
  does not preserve old addresses. What would change it: he asks for a domain
  plan after he has seen the demo.
- **The v2 site.** Enterprise AI and construction as equal audiences, Training,
  a résumé, C2C, Solutions and Demos as the navigation, and the headline
  "AI Product Leader. Systems Designer. Hands-On Builder." Those stay in
  [justinyurasits-com/](../justinyurasits-com/). This pilot does not edit those
  docs and does not build on the `v2` branch.
- **A Construction OS marketing site.** The six pain points, the offer fees,
  and Audit / Build / Monthly as the subject of the page. The product site on
  `main` keeps them. This site links to it.
- **The job evaluator**, until he says he wants it here. It is a working agent
  demo, and its subject is job postings, not construction. See
  [agents/job-evaluator](../agents/job-evaluator/).
- **Invented biography, employers, dates, metrics, clients or quotes.** Copy
  comes from this repo or from justinyurasits.com. Anything else is an `[OPEN]`
  slot.
- **Publishing a post in the first preview.** The library is there. A post
  ships when he approves the prose.
- **New visual treatments.** Colors, type and components come from brand-system
  later. The preview uses neutral styling so it does not invent a brand.

[product spec](../justinyurasits-com/product-spec.md) are the source for the
left column. This pilot does not change them.
